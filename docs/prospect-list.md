## How to fill in email and phone yourself (5 minutes per shop)
1. Search the shop name on Google Maps. The phone number and website are on the listing. That is the owner's public business number.
2. Open their website or Instagram. Look for a contact email in the footer, "Contact" page, or the Instagram bio.
3. Shops that only use Booksy/Fresha/Squire usually have no email. Use their Instagram DM, or walk in.
4. Write what you found in the table.

## About texting them
Cold TEXTING a business is riskier than it looks. US law (the TCPA) restricts marketing texts to cell phones without permission, and many shop numbers are the owner's cell. Safer order: walk in, call the shop's public number, email, or Instagram DM. If you do text, send a single friendly one-to-one message and stop if they say stop.
