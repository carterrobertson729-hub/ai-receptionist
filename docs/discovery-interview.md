# Discovery interview for prospects (15 to 25 minutes)

Goal: learn what hurts, what they want a voice agent to do, and whether they would pay. Take notes in the prospect's own words. Do not pitch until the end.

## A. Their calls today
1. About how many calls do you get in a month? How many in the day, how many at night or on weekends?
2. About how many of those go unanswered (voicemail, busy, after hours)? How do you know?
3. What happens to a missed call today? Who calls back, and how fast?
4. How long does a typical call last? How long does a booking call last?
5. When did you last lose a job because nobody picked up? What was it worth?
6. What is your average job worth? Your best jobs?
7. Of the people who call, about how many end up booking?
8. What are the busiest days or seasons? Any times you get a flood of calls (storms, cold snaps, heat waves)?

## B. Who answers the phone now
9. Who answers today (you, a spouse, a receptionist, an answering service)? What does it cost?
10. What do you like and dislike about how calls are handled now?
11. Do you use call forwarding, a business line or a cell phone? Which carrier?
12. Do you have a dispatcher, or do techs answer from the truck?

## C. Jobs, time and prices (your original list)
13. List your 8 to 12 most common job types.
14. For each: how long does it usually take, and how long when it goes badly (use that for padding)?
15. How much time do your techs need between jobs (driving, setup)?
16. Which hours and days should be bookable? Same day or next day only?
17. Which jobs would you rather not book by phone (big installs, multi-day work)? Send those to a callback or an estimate visit?
18. What is the flat price for each job? Which jobs get quoted on site?
19. Do you charge a service call fee? Different prices at night or weekends?
20. For your biggest jobs: starting price, typical range, is the estimate free?
21. How many technicians or crews can work at once?

## D. Emergencies
22. What counts as an emergency for you? Do you offer 24/7?
23. Who is on call, and how do they get alerted today (call, text, app)? What if they don't answer?
24. Is there a surcharge for emergencies?
25. Which calls should the AI always get a human on (gas, flooding, angry customers, commercial accounts)?

## E. Rules and policies
26. Do you charge for late cancellations or no-shows? How much, how late?
27. Which areas or zip codes do you serve? Any you refuse?
28. Which jobs do you NOT do?
29. Anything the AI should never say or promise?
30. Do you work with warranties, insurance claims, landlords or property managers who need special handling?

## F. Their tools
31. Where do you keep your schedule (Google Calendar, Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, paper)? Who enters appointments and how?
32. Do you use a CRM or invoicing software? What do you wish connected?
33. Do you already text customers (confirmations, reminders, "on the way")?
34. Do you take payments over the phone?

## G. What they want from a voice agent
35. If a phone assistant could do ONE thing perfectly, what would it be?
36. What would make you not trust it? What would worry you?
37. Do you want it to book, take messages, or both? Quote prices, or only say a technician will confirm?
38. Do you want to hear every call, read summaries, or only get alerts for important ones? Email, text or both?
39. Do your customers speak languages other than English?
40. Should it transfer to a person when asked, and to whom, during what hours?
41. How should it sound (formal, friendly, short)? Any phrases you hate?
42. Should callers be told it is an automated assistant? (Recommended yes.)

## H. Buying
43. Who decides on a purchase like this? Anyone else need to agree?
44. What are you spending now on missed calls, an answering service or a receptionist?
45. What monthly price feels fair? At what price does it become a no-brainer? At what price is it too much?
46. What would you need to see in a trial to say yes? How long would a trial need to run?
47. Have you tried any AI or answering service before? What happened?
48. If this worked, what would it be worth to you in a month?

## I. Wrap up
49. Can I listen to a few of your recent missed-call voicemails or call logs (names removed) to build a realistic demo?
50. Can I call you back with a demo built on YOUR job list and prices?
51. Who else do you know who has the same problem?

## After each interview
Write down: calls per month, % missed, average job value, tools used, top 3 wants, price they named, objections, next step agreed. Compare across 10 interviews to find what to build and what to charge.
