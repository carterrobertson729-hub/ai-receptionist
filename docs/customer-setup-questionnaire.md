## 5. Prices

18. Which jobs have a firm price you're happy for the AI to say out loud?

29. Do you want the AI to tell callers to call 911 or the gas company first for gas, smoke, fire, or sparks? (We recommend always yes.)

## 9b. How calls reach the assistant
