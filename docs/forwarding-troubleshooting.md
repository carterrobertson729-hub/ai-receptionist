# When an owner can't set up call forwarding: what to do

## Before you sign them
Ask in the first conversation: "Who do you pay for your business phone, and who has the login?" Their answer tells you whether forwarding is easy, and who you need on the call. Do not promise a start date until you know.

## Problem 1: they don't know how or where
1. Do it together on a video call with screen share (or phone call while they read you the menu). Look for "call forwarding", "unanswered", "no answer", "busy", "business hours".
2. Give them the exact request to say if they call the carrier: "Please turn on conditional call forwarding for no answer and busy to this number: [AI number]" (or "forward all calls" for main-line setups). You can stay on a three-way call.
3. Use the steps in docs/phone-setup-modes.md and the iPhone/Samsung/computer notes.

## Problem 2: they don't have access (someone else holds the account)
1. Find the account holder (a spouse, a business partner, an office manager, a landlord, an IT company, a franchise office).
2. Ask the account holder to join the call, or to add the owner as an authorized user.
3. For a business phone system, the company that installed it can usually make the change in minutes.

## Problem 3: their carrier or plan can't forward (or the setting is missing)
Options, easiest first:
1. Try the dial codes (`**61*number**20#` and similar; Verizon `*71`/`*72`) in case the menu hides the option.
2. Ask the carrier whether it can enable forwarding; some landlines charge a small fee.
3. Get the shop a separate business number from an internet phone service (for example Google Voice, OpenPhone, RingCentral, or a number from your own provider), put THAT number on the website and Google, and let that service forward unanswered calls to the AI. This also keeps personal calls apart.
4. Later and bigger: port the shop's number to a provider you control. Do not start here.

## Problem 4: the service asks to verify the AI number with a code
Some services call the forwarding number and ask the person who answers to type a code. The AI number cannot do that. Try another forwarding method (dial codes, the carrier's own menu), or a different phone service.

## Problem 5: they are nervous it will break their phone
- Say it is one setting and can be turned off in a minute (give them the off code or the toggle).
- Test with them on the call: you call their number, they let it ring, the AI answers; then show them how to turn it off.
- Offer a trial: forwarding on for the weekend or one week.

## Problem 6: forwarding works but something is odd
- AI never picks up: the ring time may be too long, voicemail may be answering first (the forwarding code replaces it; recheck), or Do Not Disturb is on.
- AI picks up too fast: the ring time is too short; raise it (about 4 rings, 20 seconds).
- The wrong business answers: the Retell number is attached to the wrong agent.
- A transfer loops back: the transfer number forwards to the AI; use a different number.

## If nothing works
Fallbacks that still deliver value:
- After-hours only: the AI number is offered through the shop's voicemail greeting or a message on the website ("After hours, call [AI number]"). It changes the customer's habits, so it is a last resort.
- Pause the setup until their phone situation is sorted, rather than selling something that is not live.

## Charge for it
Include phone setup help in your setup fee. If it takes more than about 30 minutes, tell them up front what you will charge for extra time.
