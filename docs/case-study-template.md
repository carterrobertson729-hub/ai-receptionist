# Case studies and testimonials (prepared 2026-11-10)

Real results from a real shop are your best sales material. This is how to collect them honestly.

## 1. Get permission first (in writing)
Ask for a reply by email. Plain wording to use:
> "May we share a short summary of how the receptionist has worked for [Business], including your business name and a quote from you, on our website and in sales emails? You can say no, and you can change your mind any time. We will show you exactly what we plan to publish before it goes out."
Keep their "yes" email. Never publish numbers, names or quotes without it. Never use a customer's call recordings in public without separate written permission from the customer AND consider the callers' privacy.

## 2. The numbers to gather (from their call log sheet)
Take them for a stated period (for example 30 days) from the Calls sheet and the weekly reports:
- Total calls the receptionist answered.
- Calls answered after hours (the shop was closed).
- Appointments booked by the receptionist.
- Messages taken, urgent calls, cancellations handled.
- Minutes used.
- A careful money estimate: bookings x their average ticket. Label it "estimated", and write down the average ticket they gave you.
Only use real numbers. A small honest number is better than a rounded one.

## 3. A short layout (about 150 words)
- **Title:** "[Shop] now catches calls it used to miss"
- **Before:** one or two sentences about how calls were handled (from their words).
- **What we set up:** plan, hours covered, how calls reach the receptionist.
- **Results:** 3 numbers with the period and "estimated" where it applies.
- **Quote:** one or two sentences from the owner, with their OK.
- **Photo or logo:** only with permission.

## 4. Questions to ask the owner (5 minutes by email or phone)
1. How were missed calls handled before? Roughly how many did you miss a week?
2. What changed after the receptionist went live?
3. What surprised you, good or bad?
4. Would you recommend it to another owner? Who?
5. What is your average ticket? (for the estimate)

## 5. Where it goes
A "Results" card on the home page, a one-page PDF you can attach to outreach emails, and a line in the day-30 onboarding email (already asks for a testimonial). Tell me when you have the numbers and permission and I'll build the card and the PDF.

## 6. Don't
- Don't invent or round up numbers, or say "up to".
- Don't publish a shop's name without a yes.
- Don't compare to competitors by name.
