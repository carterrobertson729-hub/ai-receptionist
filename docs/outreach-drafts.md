# Outreach drafts in Carter's voice (2026-11-04)

Rules for making these sound like you:
- Change anything that doesn't sound like how you talk. Short sentences, no buzzwords.
- Only add a personal line if it is TRUE ("I got my last cut at ..."). Never invent one.
- Send from your own email, sign with your real name, include your mailing address and an unsubscribe line (see the footer at the bottom).
- Send 1 to 1, not mass mail. Change the shop name and one detail for each.

## 3. If they say they called the demo
> Thanks for trying it! What did you think? Anything that sounded off or that you'd want it to say differently?

Then send the questions below (they are the "after they call" list).

## 4. Questions to send after they call (pick 5 to 8, don't send all)
Short ones you can paste in a text or email:
1. Did it sound natural, or did anything feel robotic?
2. Did it get the haircut/service and time right?
3. When your barbers are busy, what happens to the phone right now?
4. Roughly how many calls do you think you miss in a week?
5. How do most customers book today: phone, Square, walk-in, Instagram?
6. Is there anything it should never say or do on a call?
7. If this worked for your shop, what would a fair monthly price be?
8. Who else would need to be okay with it?
9. Would you want to test it for a week on your own number, to see what it catches?

**Message to send with them:**
> Thanks for calling! If you're open to it, a few quick questions would really help me make it better. Reply with whatever you feel like answering:
>
> 1. Did it sound natural?
> 2. What happens to your calls when everyone's busy?
> 3. About how many calls do you think you miss a week?
> 4. Anything you'd want it to say or never say?
> 5. Would you try it on your real line for a week?
>
> No pressure, any answers are helpful.

## 5. Replies to common answers
**"Sounds interesting, how much?"**
> It's $[plan price] a month plus a one-time $99 setup, and there's no contract, you can cancel any time. A lot of shops start on the small plan that covers about 150 to 300 minutes of calls a month. I can send the details or hop on a quick call, whichever you like.
(Check `docs/pricing-guide.md` before quoting. Don't promise free trials or a founding discount unless you've decided to offer them.)

**"Does it work with Square?"**
> Right now it books into a calendar. Connecting straight to Square is the next thing I'd build, and I'd confirm it works before promising it. Would you want that?

**"I'll think about it."**
> Totally fair. I'll check back in a week or two. Meanwhile the demo's always up at (219) 587-7319.

**"Not interested."**
> No problem, thanks for taking a look. If you know another shop owner who might like it, I'd appreciate an intro.

**"What if it messes up?"**
> Good question. It only offers times that are open, you get an email for every booking, and anything it can't handle becomes a message for you. I'd also check the first calls with you.
