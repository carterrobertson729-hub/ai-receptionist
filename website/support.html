We are here to help

Something not working, or you just want to check it? Start here. We usually reply within 2 to 3 business days. If your receptionist is not answering calls, put URGENT in the subject line and we will look at it first.

Email support Request a change

Test your receptionist in 5 minutes

  1. Use a different phone from the one that rings at your business. Call your business number and let it ring until the receptionist answers (or call after hours).
  2. Ask for a price on one of your services. It should give only the prices you gave us.
  3. Book an appointment for a time you know is free. Give a test name.
  4. Check your calendar. The appointment should be there with the name and number.
  5. Check your email. You should get a summary soon after the call. Then call again and cancel the test appointment, and make sure the time is open again.

Spotted anything wrong? Write down what you said and the time of the call, then send it with the change form.

Common problems

Calls are not reaching the receptionist

This is almost always call forwarding. Forwarding may be switched off, set to the wrong number, or turned on for the wrong situation (for example all calls instead of missed calls). Try the test above, then email us the carrier you use and what you see. We will walk you through it.

It booked the wrong time, or offered times when we are closed

Your hours, time zone or booking gap may need an update. Send the change form with the correct hours and the date and time of the call.

It said something wrong

Tell us what it said, the day and time of the call, and what it should have said. We fix the instructions and reply when it is done.

I am not getting the summary emails

Check your spam folder and mark the first one as not spam. Then tell us the email address you want the summaries sent to.

I want to change prices, hours or services

Use the change form. We make the change and confirm by email.

Billing questions, or I want to cancel

You can cancel anytime. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you already paid for, then it does not renew. Email us and we will confirm. Your receipt email from Stripe has the details of each payment.

Terms and policies

Plans, minutes and extra minutes

Plans are billed monthly or yearly (yearly is 10% less than twelve monthly payments, paid up front). Each plan covers one location and the included minutes shown. Minutes do not roll over. If you use more than your included minutes, calls keep being answered and extra minutes are billed at $0.40 each on your next bill. Add-ons are billed monthly.

Cancellation and refunds

You can cancel anytime. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for, then it does not renew. We do not refund payments for time you have already been billed for. Yearly plans are paid up front for twelve months, and cancelling stops the renewal.

Works-or-refund guarantee

If we cannot get your receptionist working with your phone line and calendar after setup, we refund what you paid. This guarantee covers the service not working. It does not cover a change of mind.

Privacy and call recording

Calls are answered by an automated assistant. Calls may be recorded and transcribed so that we can book appointments, send you summaries and keep a call log. We use call information only to provide the service to you and we do not sell it. Businesses are responsible for following the recording and disclosure laws that apply to their customers. You can ask us to delete your data when you cancel.

Emails and notifications

When you sign up you can tick an optional box to allow us to email you about your account, product updates and offers. If you tick it, we may send you those emails. If you do not, that is fine: we will still send the messages needed to run your service, such as receipts, setup questions, usage and billing notices, and summaries you ask for. You can stop the optional emails at any time by replying "unsubscribe" or emailing us.

Accounts and sign-in

You can create an account by signing in with Google. We receive your name and email address from Google, and never your Google password. We use them to set up your account, contact you about your service and, if you tick the optional box, send you updates and offers. We keep a record that you agreed to these terms. You can ask us to delete your account by emailing us.
