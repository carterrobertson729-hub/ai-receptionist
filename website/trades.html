What it handles

Built for how trades take calls

Your job types, prices, emergency rules and hours go into the setup, so callers get your answers.

Sorts emergencies from routine calls

You decide what counts as an emergency, such as a burst pipe or sewage backup. The receptionist marks those calls urgent.

Safety first

For things like a gas smell, sparking, smoke or flooding near electrics, it tells the caller to get to safety and call 911 or the gas company before it takes any details.

Alerts your on-call person

Urgent calls are emailed to the person on call and to you, with the address, the problem and the callback number. Text and phone alerts are planned.

Books visits by job type

Each job type has its own length, with time between jobs. It collects the name, address, phone and problem, and books it into your calendar.

Prices only as you set them

A firm price when you have one, a service call fee when you do not, and "we quote on site" for the rest. It never makes up a number.

Big jobs, handled carefully

For water heaters, sewer lines and similar jobs it shares only a typical range, then offers an estimate visit or a callback. Multi-day jobs are never booked as the job.

Good to know: it alerts by email today, not by text or phone call, and it does not dispatch or route your trucks. Today it books into Google Calendar. Tell us what scheduling software you use, such as Jobber or Housecall Pro, and we will tell you honestly where that stands.

FAQ

Plumbing and trades questions

What if a caller smells gas?

The receptionist tells them to get to safety and call 911 or the gas company before it asks for any details. Only then does it take their information.

Does it dispatch my technician?

No. It alerts your on-call person by email with the details, and you decide who goes. It does not route trucks.

Will it quote a price?

Only the prices you give it. For jobs you quote on site, it says so. For big jobs it shares a typical range at most, then offers an estimate visit or a callback.

Can it answer only after hours?

Yes. You can forward every call, or only the calls you miss, and you can switch forwarding off any time.

Which calendar does it use?

Google Calendar today. Tell us what you use, such as Jobber or Housecall Pro, and we will tell you where that connection stands.

What does it cost?

Plans start at $59 a month for after-hours and missed calls, or $119 for a receptionist that answers every call, and your first 50 minutes are free. See the pricing for all plans.

Terms and policies

Free trial

After setup, the first 50 minutes of calls on your receptionist are free. No payment is needed to start the trial. When the trial minutes are used, you choose a paid plan to continue. The one-time setup fee applies when you choose a plan, unless you are one of the first 3 founding customers.

Plans, minutes and extra minutes

Plans are billed monthly or yearly (yearly is 10% less than twelve monthly payments, paid up front). Each plan covers one location and the included minutes shown. Minutes do not roll over. If you use more than your included minutes, calls keep being answered and extra minutes are billed at $0.40 each on your next bill. Add-ons are billed monthly.

Cancellation and refunds

You can cancel anytime. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for, then it does not renew. We do not refund payments for time you have already been billed for. Yearly plans are paid up front for twelve months, and cancelling stops the renewal.

Works-or-refund guarantee

If we cannot get your receptionist working with your phone line and calendar after setup, we refund what you paid. This guarantee covers the service not working. It does not cover a change of mind.

Privacy and call recording

Calls are answered by an automated assistant. Calls may be recorded and transcribed so that we can book appointments, send you summaries and keep a call log. We use call information only to provide the service to you and we do not sell it. Businesses are responsible for following the recording and disclosure laws that apply to their customers. You can ask us to delete your data when you cancel.
