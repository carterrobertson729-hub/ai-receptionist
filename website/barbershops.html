What it handles

Built for how a barbershop takes calls

Your services, prices, hours and rules go into the setup, so callers get your answers, not generic ones.

Books by service

Haircuts, fades, shaves and more, each with its own length. Combos like a haircut plus a beard trim add up to the right amount of time.

Notes the barber a client asks for

It records the barber the client wants and passes it on as a request. It never promises a barber, because your shop makes that call.

Handles several barbers at once

You set how many appointments can run at the same time, so it never books more than your chairs can handle.

Parents and kids

A parent can book a child and themselves in the same call, under one phone number, with the right service for each.

Answers the usual questions

Prices, hours, address, walk-ins and card payments, only from what you gave it. It never takes card numbers over the phone.

Cancels, reschedules and checks

Callers can change their appointment by phone after giving their name and phone number, and your calendar updates.

Good to know: today it books into Google Calendar. If your shop uses Square, Boulevard or another booking app, tell us. Those connections are on our roadmap, and we will tell you honestly where they stand. Text-message reminders are not available yet.

FAQ

Barbershop questions

Do you handle walk-ins?

The receptionist tells callers that walk-ins are welcome if that is your policy. It will not promise a wait time, because it cannot know one. Callers who want a set time can book.

What if a client wants a specific barber?

It notes the request and passes it on. Your shop confirms it, so a barber who is off that day never gets promised.

Can it take payments?

No. It never takes card numbers over the phone. Payment stays at your shop.

Does it speak Spanish?

Yes, with the Spanish add-on. It starts in English and switches when the caller speaks Spanish. We test it with your own services first.

I use Square or another booking app. Will it work?

Today we book into Google Calendar, so a shop on Square would have two places to check. Direct connections to Square and other apps are planned. Tell us what you use and we will give you a straight answer.

What does it cost?

Plans start at $59 a month for after-hours and missed calls, or $119 for a receptionist that answers every call. See the pricing for all plans.

Terms and policies

Plans, minutes and extra minutes

Plans are billed monthly or yearly (yearly is 10% less than twelve monthly payments, paid up front). Each plan covers one location and the included minutes shown. Minutes do not roll over. If you use more than your included minutes, calls keep being answered and extra minutes are billed at $0.40 each on your next bill. Add-ons are billed monthly.

Cancellation and refunds

You can cancel anytime. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for, then it does not renew. We do not refund payments for time you have already been billed for. Yearly plans are paid up front for twelve months, and cancelling stops the renewal.

Works-or-refund guarantee

If we cannot get your receptionist working with your phone line and calendar after setup, we refund what you paid. This guarantee covers the service not working. It does not cover a change of mind.

Privacy and call recording

Calls are answered by an automated assistant. Calls may be recorded and transcribed so that we can book appointments, send you summaries and keep a call log. We use call information only to provide the service to you and we do not sell it. Businesses are responsible for following the recording and disclosure laws that apply to their customers. You can ask us to delete your data when you cancel.

Emails and notifications

When you sign up you can tick an optional box to allow us to email you about your account, product updates and offers. If you tick it, we may send you those emails. If you do not, that is fine: we will still send the messages needed to run your service, such as receipts, setup questions, usage and billing notices, and summaries you ask for. You can stop the optional emails at any time by replying "unsubscribe" or emailing us.

Accounts and sign-in

You can create an account by signing in with Google. We receive your name and email address from Google, and never your Google password. We use them to set up your account, contact you about your service and, if you tick the optional box, send you updates and offers. We keep a record that you agreed to these terms. You can ask us to delete your account by emailing us.
