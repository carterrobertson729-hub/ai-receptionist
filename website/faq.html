Questions owners ask

Straight answers about setup, phones, pricing and what the receptionist can and cannot do.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. You keep your number. You turn on call forwarding to the number we give you. You can forward only the calls you miss, or every call, and you can turn forwarding off in seconds.

What happens when I sign up? When do I get my phone number?

You fill in a short setup form. We build your receptionist, set up a phone number for it, and test it. Then we email you the number with step-by-step instructions for your phone company. Most setups take about a week. You turn on forwarding, we run a final test call, and you are live.

How is this different from voicemail?

Voicemail takes a message and the caller often hangs up. The receptionist talks to the caller, answers their questions, books the appointment and tells you what happened.

Which calendar does it use?

It books into Google Calendar today. Support for other booking systems, such as Square, is on our roadmap. Tell us what you use and we will tell you where it stands.

Will callers know it is an automated assistant?

Yes. It says it is an automated assistant if asked, and your greeting can say so. You are responsible for following the call recording and disclosure rules that apply where your customers are, and we help you set the greeting.

What if it cannot help a caller?

It takes a message or schedules a callback and tells you about it. It does not make up prices or answers. If it does not know something, it says so.

Does it take payments?

No. It never takes card numbers over the phone. Payment stays with your business.

I have more than one location. Can I add another?

Yes. Add an extra number for $15 a month for the second location. It shares your plan's minutes. If that location has different hours, services or a different calendar, tell us and we will set it up for it.

I own several locations. Do you have a bigger plan?

Yes. For multiple locations or more than 1,200 minutes a month we build a custom plan: a large pool of minutes shared across your locations, a phone number for each one, and a price that fits your volume. Contact sales and we will put it together.

Can it speak Spanish?

Yes, with the Spanish add-on. It starts in English and switches when the caller speaks Spanish. We test it with your own services before you rely on it.

What happens if I use more minutes than my plan?

The receptionist keeps answering. Extra minutes are $0.40 each and are added to the next bill. We email you at 80% and 100% of your minutes, and if you go over often we will suggest a bigger plan, which costs less per minute.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for (the month, or the year on a yearly plan). Time already paid for is not refunded. See the terms at the bottom of the page for details.

What if it does not work with my phone setup?

If we cannot get your receptionist working with your phone line and calendar, we refund what you paid. This covers the service not working, not a change of mind.

Is my call information safe?

We use call information only to provide the service to you and we do not sell it. Calls may be recorded and transcribed so we can book appointments, send summaries and keep a call log. You can ask us to delete your data when you cancel.

Terms and policies

Plans, minutes and extra minutes

Plans are billed monthly or yearly (yearly is 10% less than twelve monthly payments, paid up front). Each plan covers one location and the included minutes shown. Minutes do not roll over. If you use more than your included minutes, calls keep being answered and extra minutes are billed at $0.40 each on your next bill. Add-ons are billed monthly.

Cancellation and refunds

You can cancel anytime. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for, then it does not renew. We do not refund payments for time you have already been billed for. Yearly plans are paid up front for twelve months, and cancelling stops the renewal.

Works-or-refund guarantee

If we cannot get your receptionist working with your phone line and calendar after setup, we refund what you paid. This guarantee covers the service not working. It does not cover a change of mind.

Privacy and call recording

Calls are answered by an automated assistant. Calls may be recorded and transcribed so that we can book appointments, send you summaries and keep a call log. We use call information only to provide the service to you and we do not sell it. Businesses are responsible for following the recording and disclosure laws that apply to their customers. You can ask us to delete your data when you cancel.

Emails and notifications

When you sign up you can tick an optional box to allow us to email you about your account, product updates and offers. If you tick it, we may send you those emails. If you do not, that is fine: we will still send the messages needed to run your service, such as receipts, setup questions, usage and billing notices, and summaries you ask for. You can stop the optional emails at any time by replying "unsubscribe" or emailing us.

Accounts and sign-in

You can create an account by signing in with Google. We receive your name and email address from Google, and never your Google password. We use them to set up your account, contact you about your service and, if you tick the optional box, send you updates and offers. We keep a record that you agreed to these terms. You can ask us to delete your account by emailing us.
