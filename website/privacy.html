Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Who we are

AnswerBay provides an automated AI phone receptionist for local businesses. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have. Questions: carter.robertson729@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your account. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google. We never receive your Google password.

Forms on this website. When you book a demo, contact us, place an order or fill in the setup form, we receive what you type, such as your name, email, phone number, business name, hours, services, prices and calendar details. These forms are collected through Netlify, our website host.

Calls to your receptionist. Calls are answered by an automated assistant. We process the call audio, a written transcript, and the details the caller gives (such as name, phone number, the service they want and the time they choose), plus the length of the call.

Payments. Payments are handled by Stripe. We do not see or store full card numbers. We receive your plan, billing email and payment status.

Google user data (Sign in with Google)

What we access. When you choose "Continue with Google", AnswerBay receives only your name and email address from your Google account (and the account identifier Google uses to sign you in). We do not ask for, and do not receive, your Google password, your contacts, your emails, your Drive files or your calendar through Google sign-in.

How we use it. We use your name and email only to create and recognise your AnswerBay account, to show your name on the site while you are signed in, to fill in our forms for you, to contact you about your service and, only if you ticked the optional box, to send you updates and offers.

How we share it. We do not sell Google user data. We do not use it for advertising and we do not give it to advertisers or data brokers. We share it only with the service providers that help us run the service (listed below), when the law requires it, or with your permission.

How we store and protect it. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. Your name and email are also kept in our records of your account and in the sign-up and order forms collected through Netlify. We limit access to the people who run AnswerBay and use reputable, secured providers.

How long we keep it and how to delete it. We keep it while you have an account with us. To delete your account and the name and email we received from Google, email carter.robertson729@gmail.com from the address on the account. We will delete it unless the law requires us to keep a record. You can also remove AnswerBay's access in your Google Account settings under "Sign in with Google".

AnswerBay's use of information received from Google follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Calendars you share with us

To book appointments for you, you may share a Google Calendar with the AnswerBay service account. We read the busy times on that calendar to offer open times, and we create, move and cancel only the appointments the receptionist books. We do not use your calendar for any other purpose and we do not share it except with the providers that run the service.

How we use it

We use your information to run the service: to set up and test your receptionist, book appointments into your calendar, email you summaries and reports, keep a call log, send receipts and billing notices, answer your questions and keep the service secure. If you tick the optional box when you sign up, we may also email you about your account, updates and offers.

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to run the service: Retell AI (the voice assistant), n8n (automation), Google (Calendar, Sheets, Gmail and Google sign-in through Firebase), Stripe (payments) and Netlify (website hosting and forms). They process information only to provide their service to us. We do not sell your personal information. We may also share information when the law requires it.

Call recording and disclosure

Calls are answered by an automated assistant. Calls may be recorded and transcribed so that we can book appointments, send you summaries and keep a call log. We use call information only to provide the service to you and we do not sell it. Businesses are responsible for following the recording and disclosure laws that apply to their customers.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep information while you are a customer and as long as we need it to provide the service and meet legal and accounting needs. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time by emailing carter.robertson729@gmail.com, and we will do so unless we are required to keep something by law.

Your choices

You can stop the optional emails at any time by replying "unsubscribe" or emailing us. You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it.

Security and children

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no system is completely secure. Our service is for businesses and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date below shows the latest version.

Terms and policies

Plans, minutes and extra minutes

Plans are billed monthly or yearly (yearly is 10% less than twelve monthly payments, paid up front). Each plan covers one location and the included minutes shown. Minutes do not roll over. If you use more than your included minutes, calls keep being answered and extra minutes are billed at $0.40 each on your next bill. Add-ons are billed monthly.

Cancellation and refunds

You can cancel anytime. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for, then it does not renew. We do not refund payments for time you have already been billed for. Yearly plans are paid up front for twelve months, and cancelling stops the renewal.

Works-or-refund guarantee

If we cannot get your receptionist working with your phone line and calendar after setup, we refund what you paid. This guarantee covers the service not working. It does not cover a change of mind.

Privacy and call recording

Calls are answered by an automated assistant. Calls may be recorded and transcribed so that we can book appointments, send you summaries and keep a call log. We use call information only to provide the service to you and we do not sell it. Businesses are responsible for following the recording and disclosure laws that apply to their customers. You can ask us to delete your data when you cancel.

Emails and notifications

When you sign up you can tick an optional box to allow us to email you about your account, product updates and offers. If you tick it, we may send you those emails. If you do not, that is fine: we will still send the messages needed to run your service, such as receipts, setup questions, usage and billing notices, and summaries you ask for. You can stop the optional emails at any time by replying "unsubscribe" or emailing us.

Accounts and sign-in

You can create an account by signing in with Google. We receive your name and email address from Google, and never your Google password. We use them to set up your account, contact you about your service and, if you tick the optional box, send you updates and offers. We keep a record that you agreed to these terms. You can ask us to delete your account by emailing us.
